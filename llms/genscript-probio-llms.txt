# GenScript ProBio (ProBio)

> GenScript ProBio is the biologics and cell & gene therapy contract development and manufacturing
> organization (CDMO) segment of GenScript Biotech Corporation (HKEX 1548). It provides end-to-end
> drug-discovery-to-commercialization services — antibody and ADC discovery, antibody engineering,
> developability assessment, cell line development, bioanalytical and bioassay services, process
> development, and cGMP clinical and commercial manufacturing of recombinant proteins, plasmid DNA,
> lentiviral and AAV vectors, mRNA and cell therapies.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: ProBio publishes NO public API. There is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no MCP server and no agent card. As of
2026-08-21 the following were probed and all missed: /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json,
/v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc, /graphql and /llms.txt all return HTTP 404; every
/.well-known/ path (security.txt, openid-configuration, oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog,
ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, agent.json) returns HTTP 403; api./developer./docs./portal.
subdomains do not resolve; no GitHub organization exists. Engagement is via a quote request form,
a regional sales email, or an authenticated customer account area. Do not attempt to call an API
here — there is nothing to call.

## Services

- Biologics discovery: antibody lead generation, hybridoma, single B cell screening, transgenic mouse fully human antibody discovery, bispecific and single-domain antibody discovery, CAR lead discovery, ADC discovery, TCR engineering.
- Antibody lead optimization: humanization, affinity maturation, Fc engineering, developability assessment.
- Biologics CDMO: cell line development, GMP cell banking, IND-enabling CMC, process development and characterization, 200-500L and 2000L drug substance manufacturing, aseptic fill and finish.
- Plasmid CDMO: GMP plasmid manufacture, linearized DNA manufacture.
- Viral vector CDMO: lentiviral vector and AAV one-stop solutions, cell banking, LVV/AAV manufacturing.
- mRNA CDMO: mRNA IND-enabling CMC, mRNA manufacture, RNA-tLNP one-stop solution.
